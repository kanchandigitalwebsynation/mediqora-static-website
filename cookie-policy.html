This Cookie Policy explains how Mediqora uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience and understand how our Services are used.

Last updated: May 20, 2024

◎

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember preferences and provide useful information about visits.

⚙

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to keep Mediqora secure, remember settings, improve performance, and understand which features are useful to our users.

▣

3. Types of Cookies

  • Essential cookies: Required for core functions and security.
  • Preference cookies: Remember choices such as language and settings.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand usage and improve the Services.
✓

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect how parts of the Services work.

✉

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Cookie Policy, contact us at privacy@mediqora.com.
